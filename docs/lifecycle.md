# SANKALP Solution Lifecycle

The core lifecycle of societal innovation in SANKALP flows through 11 distinct stages:

```
[1. Citizen] 
     ↓
[2. Problem Report] 
     ↓
[3. AI Analysis & Deduplication] 
     ↓
[4. Challenge Formulation] 
     ↓
[5. University / Student Engagement] 
     ↓
[6. Solution Development] 
     ↓
[7. Expert Evaluation & Scoring] 
     ↓
[8. Industry / CSR Partnership] 
     ↓
[9. Pilot Project Implementation] 
     ↓
[10. Field Verification & Testing] 
     ↓
[11. Sustainable Deployment]
```

---

## Detailed Lifecycle Stages

### Stage 1 & 2: Citizen Problem Reporting
- **Actors**: Citizens, Community Leaders, Local NGOs.
- **Action**: Citizens report a localized societal issue (e.g., water quality degradation, waste segregation bottlenecks, rural transport accessibility) with location, media attachments, and impact details.

### Stage 3: AI Problem Analysis
- **Service**: `server/src/services/ai/`
- **Actions**:
  1. **Categorization**: Classifies into domains (Water, Waste, Energy, Healthcare, Agriculture, Mobility, Education, Governance).
  2. **Deduplication**: Identifies similar existing issues using semantic similarity.
  3. **Prioritization**: Calculates an urgency/impact score based on affected population, safety risks, and recurring trends.

### Stage 4: Challenge Formulation
- **Actors**: Admin / AI Service.
- **Action**: Verified high-impact problems are aggregated into structured, competitive innovation **Challenges** with clear problem statements, constraints, and success metrics.

### Stage 5 & 6: University Solution Development
- **Actors**: University Faculty, Student Innovators, Research Labs.
- **Action**: Universities adopt challenges, register multidisciplinary teams, submit project proposals, design prototypes, and document architectural solutions.

### Stage 7: Expert Evaluation
- **Actors**: Certified Evaluators, Domain Specialists.
- **Action**: Blind/multi-evaluator assessment evaluating technical feasibility, novelty, budget compliance, social impact, and scalability.

### Stage 8: Industry / CSR Sponsorship & Incubation
- **Actors**: Corporates, CSR Funds, Venture Studios, Accelerators.
- **Action**: Industry partners browse evaluated top solutions to provide grants, mentorship, supply-chain access, and co-development funding.

### Stage 9 & 10: Pilot Implementation & Verification
- **Actors**: Local Government, Municipal Bodies, Universities, Industry Sponsors.
- **Action**: Structured rollout in designated pilot zones (e.g., specific ward/panchayat) with sensor telemetry, user feedback, and measurable KPIs.

### Stage 11: Large-Scale Deployment
- **Actors**: Government Agencies, Enterprise Scale-up Partners.
- **Action**: Transition from pilot to permanent regional/national deployment with transparent public impact dashboards.
